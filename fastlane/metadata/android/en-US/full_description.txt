Listen to Animikii Club's Eclectic001 Turtle Island Ojibwe Edition on Android.

You can:
- Listen in the background
- See what's playing and browse recent tracks
- Search the station's song list and send a request
- Reconnect automatically after network interruptions
- View album art when the station provides it

The app uses Animikii Club's public AzuraCast API and 128 kbps MP3 stream. It
needs an internet connection. Song requests go to the station, and album art
comes from HTTPS URLs the station provides. No account or station credentials
are needed.
